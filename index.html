Here's the truth: AI systems don't care how old you are. They care about consistency, clarity, and documented expertise. A startup can absolutely build recommendation visibility if you're systematic about it.

We track four key metrics across four AI models: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity.

  • Citation Footprint: How many times your brand appears as a cited source across relevant category questions. Example: cited in 12 of 50 questions = 24% citation footprint.
  • Answer Share: What percentage of AI-generated answers in your category mention you. Example: "You appear in 18% of all answers about X topic."
  • Prompt Presence: How often your brand appears when we ask variations of the same question. Tests consistency across models and prompts.
  • Competitive Position: How you rank against competitors. Are you gaining or losing share?

Every week, we probe each model with 20–30 questions from your domain. We track these metrics, spot trends, and adjust strategy based on what's working.
